UPSC Civil Services Mains Syllabus

Official UPSC Mains Examination Syllabus

✍ Essay Paper

  • Candidates may be required to write essays on multiple topics.
  • Credit is given for clarity of thought, effective expression and logical presentation.

📘 General Studies Paper I

  • Indian Heritage and Culture
  • Modern Indian History
  • World History
  • Indian Society
  • Geography of India and the World

⚖ General Studies Paper II

  • Indian Constitution
  • Governance
  • Parliament and State Legislatures
  • Judiciary
  • Welfare Schemes
  • Social Justice
  • International Relations

📈 General Studies Paper III

  • Indian Economy
  • Agriculture
  • Science & Technology
  • Environment and Biodiversity
  • Disaster Management
  • Internal Security

🧠 General Studies Paper IV

  • Ethics and Human Interface
  • Attitude
  • Emotional Intelligence
  • Aptitude
  • Probity in Governance
  • Case Studies

📚 Optional Subject

  • One Optional Subject selected by the candidate.
  • Optional Paper I
  • Optional Paper II

🗣 Qualifying Language Papers

  • Paper A – Indian Language (Qualifying)
  • Paper B – English (Qualifying)
